The Fortress (Blue Team Defense)

The online exam portal was built as an impenetrable zero-tolerance fortress. Every single client telemetry vector was fortified with tripwires.

If a student alt-tabs, exits fullscreen, moves their mouse outside the window, pastes text, or types faster than 15ms per keystroke, the server instantly terminates their session.

Active Threat Vector
Telemetry Listeners: ['blur', 'visibilitychange', 'fullscreenchange', 'mouseleave', 'paste', 'keydown_interval']
